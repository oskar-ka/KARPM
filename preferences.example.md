# What I am looking for

Copy this to `preferences.md` and rewrite it in your own words. The whole file
is handed to Claude with every listing, so be concrete and say what you would
tell a friend who was looking on your behalf. Contradictions and vagueness here
are the main cause of scores that feel wrong.

## The bike

- Yamaha MT-07, 2018 or newer.
- Budget: up to 6,000 €. I would stretch to 6,500 € for something exceptional.
- Under 25,000 km. Under 15,000 km is a real plus.
- A2-restrictable (35 kW) matters to me; note it if the listing mentions it.

## What I care about

- Full service history (Scheckheft) is worth more to me than low mileage.
- Unfallfrei. I will not consider anything with accident damage, however cheap.
- Fresh HU/TÜV, or at least a year remaining.
- One or two previous owners. Four owners on a six-year-old bike is a warning.
- Standard or tastefully modified. Loud aftermarket exhausts and crash cages
  are a negative unless the original parts come with it.
- A long, specific description written by someone who clearly rode the bike is
  a good sign. Three lines and four blurry photos is not.

## What I do not care about

- Colour.
- Tyre brand, as long as there is tread left.
- Whether the seller is private or a dealer, though a private seller with a
  detailed history is usually better value.

## Logistics

- I am in Hamburg. Within 200 km is easy; further needs to be worth the trip.
- I can collect at weekends.
